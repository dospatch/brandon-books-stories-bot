Welcome to Brandon Books & Stories.

I’m an independent author, storyteller, and creator who believes that the experiences we live, the people we love, and the memories we make are worth preserving.

Through books, music, videos, and other creative projects, I turn meaningful experiences and ideas into stories that can be shared, remembered, and enjoyed by others.

✍️ Meet the Author

Brandon D. Coleman Jr.

I’m Brandon D. Coleman Jr., an independent author, storyteller, and creator.

My journey into writing is deeply personal. I write because there are stories I want to tell, memories I want to preserve, and experiences that have helped shape the person I am today.

For me, becoming an author isn't simply about publishing a book. It's about taking something meaningful from my life and turning it into something that can live beyond a single moment.

Writing gives me a way to remember. Creating gives me a way to express myself. Sharing my work gives me the opportunity to connect with readers who may find something meaningful in the stories I tell.

I'm continuing to learn, grow, and build my career as an author — one story, one project, and one chapter at a time.
