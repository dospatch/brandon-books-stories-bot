MEET THE AUTHOR

Welcome to my story.

I'm Brandon D. Coleman Jr., an author and storyteller who enjoys putting memories and experiences into words and sharing meaningful stories with others.

This website is a home for my books, writing updates, personal stories, creative projects, and the journey behind the work.
