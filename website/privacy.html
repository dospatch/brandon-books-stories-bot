Privacy information

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Information you choose to provide

If you submit a reader review, the form may send the name you enter, selected book, rating, review text, optional favorite part, and optional Discord username to the Brandon Books & Stories review team through the connected Discord community. The review form does not require a Discord username.

Local app preferences

The app may save your selected light or dark theme and reader favorites in your browser's local storage on the device you use. These preferences are not an account and are not automatically synchronized between devices.

Offline support

If you install the app or visit it with a supported browser, its service worker may cache selected public website pages and files on your device so previously visited content can work offline. Retailer availability and external pages require an internet connection.

External websites

Book links may open Amazon, Barnes & Noble, TikTok Shop, Discord, or social-media websites. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Brandon Books & Stories does not process book payments in this app.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted on Vercel. The hosting provider may process technical request information as part of delivering and securing the website. Consult the provider's privacy information for details about its processing.

Questions

For questions about this privacy information, contact the Brandon Books & Stories team through the official Discord community.
