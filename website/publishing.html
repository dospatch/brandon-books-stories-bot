THE AUTHOR JOURNEY

A clear process, from first hello to a published listing.

These are the planned stages. The submission portal will open only after secure accounts, private file storage, and review tools are ready.

  1. 01

    Author profile

    Share your author name, contact details, background, and the kind of work you create.

  2. 02

    Project information

    Provide a working title, category, short synopsis, intended audience, and publication goals.

  3. 03

    Editorial review

    The publishing team reviews the proposal and communicates any questions or next steps.

  4. 04

    Agreement and preparation

    If a project is selected, expectations, rights, responsibilities, costs (if any), and timelines must be documented before work begins.

  5. 05

    Publication and promotion

    Approved books can receive an author profile and catalog page with verified retailer destinations.

Author rights matter. Authors retain ownership of their work unless a separate written agreement explicitly says otherwise. No rights are transferred by visiting this page or contacting the team.
